- **RECONNECT**: "Write a nostalgic but forward-looking message. If there's shared history (previous conversations, mutual connections), reference it. Frame as a warm re-entry, not a cold outreach."
- **FUTURE_PIVOT**: "Write a very brief, light-touch message. No pressure, no ask. Just planting a seed and keeping the door open. Keep it to 2-3 sentences maximum."
